About the game

Every level is a theme like Beach Day, Snow Day, Party Time or Princess Tea. Two cards pop up at a time, so tap the one that fits the theme and watch it fly onto the doll. Pick hair, tops, skirts, shoes, bags, hats, sparkles and even the photo spot in up to eight quick choices. Then snap a photo and see the bunny, kitty and bear judges hold up their scores.

  • Fast and fun this or that choices for kids
  • Eight cute themes with painted photo spots
  • Tops, skirts, shoes, bags, hats and hairstyles to mix
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
