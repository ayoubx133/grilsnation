Babysitter Busy Day

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Feed, bathe, play with and rock a sweet baby through a busy day!

About the game

A cute baby shows what she needs in little thought bubbles. Pick the right tool and care for her with your finger: hold the bottle, pat for a burp, scrub bath bubbles, swap a fresh diaper, shake the rattle and rock her to sleep with a music box lullaby. Help her before the bubble ring runs out to keep her happy all day. Earn coins to dress her in new onesie colors.

  • Six care activities with real finger actions
  • Gentle timers and a happy meter, never a fail
  • Six cute onesie colors to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
