Friendship Bracelet Beads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

String cute beads, spell names with letter beads and gift bracelets to friends.

About the game

Read your friend's pattern card, pick a pretty cord and tap or drag beads onto the string in the right order. Spell words like LOVE and BFF with letter beads, add kitty, flower and pearl beads, then drag the cord end round to close the bracelet and tap to tie the knot. Hang a sparkly charm, gift it and see your friend wear it in a cute photo. Earn coins to unlock glossy, pearly, glitter, candy, crystal and jelly bead styles.

  • String beads by pattern with satisfying click sounds
  • Letter beads, kitty beads and sparkly charms
  • Six bead styles to collect
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
