About the game

Guests visit your henna corner in a sunny courtyard with a card of the design they love. Choose the pattern from the pattern book, pick the right henna cone and trace the dotted flowers, vines, paisley and mandalas on the palm and fingers. Add dot accents and gems, fan the paste dry, then rub it off to reveal the warm orange and brown art.

  • Trace mandala, paisley, flower and vine patterns
  • Dry and peel the paste for a magic reveal
  • Many guest cards and six decorated cones
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
