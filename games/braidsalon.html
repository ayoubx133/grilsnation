Braid Master Salon

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Brush, section and braid hair with your finger, then add ribbons and pearls.

About the game

Your client sits in the salon chair and shows you the braid she wants. Brush out the tangles, tap the sparkles to split her hair into strands, then swipe left and right on the beat to cross the strands and watch the braid grow. Make classic, fishtail, double and crown braids, tie a ribbon in her favorite color and drag pearls, flowers or stars onto the braid. Snap a photo and see how many stars you earn!

  • Real braiding with left and right swipes
  • Classic, fishtail, double and crown braids
  • Ribbons, pearls, flowers and six hair colors
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
