About the game

Every show has a theme card with wishes like a pink outfit, long hair or a sparkly tiara. Pick hairstyles, runway outfits, shoes, tiaras, jewels, bags and heart glasses, then tap WALK. Your model walks down the runway toward the cameras, so tap when she steps on a glowing spot to twirl, jump or strike a heart pose. The bunny, kitty and bear judges score her style and her poses.

  • Dress up with sparkly runway outfits and accessories
  • Tap in time on the glowing spots to pose
  • Six runway light colors to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
