Dress a cute anime princess for every royal ball and win stars from the judges!

About the game

Every ball has a theme card with the guests' wishes, like a blue gown, long curls or a snowflake tiara. Pick hairstyles, gowns, tiaras, jewels, shoes and a pretty fan by tapping them or dragging them onto the princess. Tap DONE to take her photo in the palace ballroom and watch the cute bunny, kitty and bear judges hold up their scores. Earn coins to unlock new ballrooms such as the Ice Palace, the Garden and the Starry Hall.

  • 24 sparkly anime gowns and hairstyles plus tiaras, jewels and shoes
  • Fun theme wishes with friendly critter judges
  • A painted palace ballroom and five more halls to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
