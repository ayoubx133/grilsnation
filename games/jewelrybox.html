Design sparkly necklaces, rings, earrings and tiaras with shiny gems.

About the game

Read the customer order and pick gold, silver or rose metal for the necklace, ring, earrings, bracelet, tiara or choker. Tap or drag the right gems into each empty setting, tap the gems to lock the prongs and hang a cute charm. Rub the piece with the polishing cloth until it sparkles, then drop it into a gift box for a photo reveal. Every piece you make fills your jewelry box, and coins unlock new box designs.

  • Set real looking gems into necklaces, rings and tiaras
  • Polish, lock and gift wrap every piece
  • Fill your own jewelry box collection
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
