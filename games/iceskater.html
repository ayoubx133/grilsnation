About the game

Every ice show has a theme card with wishes like a blue skating dress, pink skates or fluffy earmuffs. Pick hairstyles, sparkly skating dresses, skates, earmuffs and scarves, then tap SKATE to start the show. Your skater glides around the rink to an elegant waltz and glowing rings appear where she is going: tap when she reaches a ring to make her spin, jump and twirl. The bunny, kitty and bear judges score your look and your skating, and coins unlock new sparkle trails.

  • Sparkly skating dresses, skates, earmuffs and scarves
  • Tap in time with the music to spin, jump and twirl
  • A glittering ice rink and six sparkle trails to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
