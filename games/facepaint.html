About the game

Kids line up at your fair booth with a wish card for their face paint. Trace the dotted design with your brush, rub bright colors inside the lines, shake sparkly glitter and stick shiny gems. Then the photo booth snaps a before and after picture of the happy kid.

  • Butterfly, kitty, rainbow, unicorn and many more designs
  • Trace, color, glitter and gems with your finger
  • Six magic brushes to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
