About the game

Spend a magical day at the unicorn stable with your own cute unicorn. Sweep the hay off the floor, scrub away the mud and watch your unicorn sparkle clean right where you rub, then paint a rainbow mane with the magic brush or braid it by swiping left and right. Polish the golden horn and hooves until they shine, feed sparkly magic apples and stroke your unicorn to sleep under the stars. Finish each task list to earn coins and unlock pink, lavender, mint, peach and sky blue unicorns.

  • Wash, braid, rainbow paint and polish with your finger
  • Six unicorn colors to unlock
  • Magic apples, sparkles and sweet dream bedtime
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
