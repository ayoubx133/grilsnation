About the game

Every school day has a theme written on the chalkboard, like Sports Day, Art Class or Picture Day, plus a short to do list. Pick hairstyles, school uniforms, shoes, backpacks and hair clips by tapping them or dragging them onto the student. Tap DONE, smile for the class photo and watch the report card fill up with A+ stamps. Earn coins to unlock new classroom moods like Sakura, Sunset, Snow Day and a Starry night.

  • Nine school looks: sailor, blazer, gym, art smock, picture day dress and more
  • A new theme of the day on the chalkboard with a fun to do list
  • Class photo and a report card full of A+ stamps
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
