Kitten Care Cottage

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Feed, bathe, play with and tuck in cute kittens in a cozy pastel cottage.

About the game

Every day your kittens need care: watch the needs bars for food, bath, play, nap and love. Tap the bowl to feed, rub in bubbles and rinse with the shower, towel dry the fur, drag the yarn ball or wave the feather so your kitten pounces, and brush the fur for happy purrs. Stroke your kitten to sleep in the comfy cat bed and fill every bar to finish the day. Adopt a new kitten every few days and unlock cute bows, collars and crowns.

  • Five needs bars to fill with real finger care
  • Six cute kittens to adopt in your cottage
  • Bows, collars, flowers and crowns to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
