About the game

Every level has a magic card with wishes, like a moon outfit, a heart wand or angel wings. Dress your magical girl with sparkly outfits, wands, wings, crowns and hairstyles, then tap MAGIC. Tap tap tap to charge the sparkle and watch her spin into her magical look. Then tap the grumpy rain clouds so her wand sends sparkles that turn them into happy clouds with rainbows.

  • Six magical outfits plus wands, wings, crowns and many hairstyles
  • Tap to transform with a spinning sparkle show
  • Cheer up grumpy rain clouds and fill the sky with rainbows
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
