Paint, file and decorate cute anime nails to match every client's order!

About the game

Every client brings an order card with her favorite polish color, a sticker and sometimes glitter or one special ring nail. Soak the fingertips in a bubbly bowl, file the rough tips smooth, then paint each nail with your finger and the polish stays neatly inside the lines. Add sparkly glitter, drag cute stickers like hearts, stars, cherries and crowns onto the nails and dry them with a little fan. Snap a photo of the finished hand, watch the order checklist tick and earn coins to unlock new cushion mats and cozy sweater sleeves.

  • Six glossy polish colors, glitter and twelve cute nail stickers
  • Soak, file, paint, decorate and dry with your finger
  • Client orders with stars and six cute mats to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
