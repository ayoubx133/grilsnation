About the game

Decorate an empty cafe with tables, booth sofas, a counter, cake cases, wall art, lamps and plants to match the theme card, and choose their colors. Then open the cafe: guests sit down and order lattes, tea, cakes, cupcakes, macarons and donuts. Tap the treat they want to serve it and collect tips. Use your coins to unlock new decor and new cafe colors.

  • Decorate your own pastel cafe
  • Serve drinks and sweets to cute guests
  • Earn tips to unlock new decor
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
