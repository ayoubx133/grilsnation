About the game

Design the aquarium of your dreams! First rub the green algae off the glass with a sponge until it sparkles. Then drag castles, coral, seaweed, shells, a treasure chest, a little diver and more onto the sand to match the tank wish. Tap the fish bags and watch clownfish, goldfish, bettas, a sea turtle, a seahorse, a pufferfish and other sea friends splash in and swim around. Tap the water to shake in food flakes and feed every fish until they are happy and full, then take a photo of your tank. Every order brings a new friend for your fish book, and coins unlock six water themes from pink lagoon to starry night.

  • Clean, decorate, add fish and feed in every tank
  • Twelve cute sea friends to collect in the fish book
  • Six water themes and twelve decorations to mix
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
