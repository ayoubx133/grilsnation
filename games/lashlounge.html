About the game

Every client brings a card with the lash style and colors she wants. Swipe away the old lashes, choose natural, doll, cat eye or wispy lashes and place each extension with your tweezers on the glowing dots. Curl them up, pluck the stray brow hairs, brush on brow color and dreamy eyeshadow. Then reveal the before and after in the magic hand mirror.

  • Lash by lash placement with tweezers
  • Brow shaping, brow color and eyeshadow
  • Many client cards and six tweezer sets to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
