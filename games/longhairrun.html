About the game

Swipe left and right to switch lanes on the candy road and collect silky hair locks so your hair grows longer and longer. Dodge the snipping scissors and the sticky bubble gum, and run through the shower arches for an extra boost. At the finish the tape measure turns your hair length into a coin bonus. Then pick a cute style: twin tails, a braid or a ponytail!

  • Hair that really grows as you run
  • Scissors, gum and shower arches
  • Six runners with different hair colors
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
