Make sparkly temporary glitter tattoos with stencils, glue and gems!

About the game

Customers bring order cards for the glitter tattoo they want. Drag the stencil onto the arm and rub out the bubbles, brush glue into the holes and shake glitter in the right colors. Dust off the loose sparkles, peel the stencil to reveal the design, add shiny gems and spray a sealing mist.

  • Butterflies, hearts, unicorns, rainbows and more
  • Real stencil, glue, glitter and peel steps
  • Six stencil sheet colors to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
