Scrunchie Sew Shop

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Cut, sew and scrunch cute fabric scrunchies, then style a doll's ponytail.

About the game

Read the customer order and pick the right fabric, from polka dots and gingham to hearts, stars and satin. Drag the scissors along the dotted line, trace the stitches to sew the tube and pull the elastic through with a safety pin as the fabric gathers up. Tap to scrunch it into a ring, add a bow, pearls or a flower and slide it onto a doll's ponytail for a photo. Earn coins to unlock new scissors and thread colors.

  • Cut, sew and gather real looking fabric
  • Eight fabric patterns in seven colors
  • Style the finished scrunchie on a doll
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
