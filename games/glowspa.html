Glow Spa Day

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Give a calm, glowy face spa with your finger: steam, scrub, masks and gems!

About the game

Each client brings a spa menu with the steps she booked and her wishes, like a lilac peel mask, gold eye patches or star face gems. Wipe the face clean with a cotton pad, hold the warm steamer, scrub in gentle little circles, then paint on a mask and peel it off slowly. Dab and rub in the moisturizer, drag eye patches into place and stick on sparkly gems. Relaxing music and soft sounds make every spa day calm and cozy.

  • Seven soothing spa steps all done with your finger
  • Satisfying peel off masks, eye patches and sparkly face gems
  • Calm music and six spa moods to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
