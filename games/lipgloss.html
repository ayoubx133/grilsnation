About the game

Run your own cute beauty lab and make lip gloss for every customer. Pour the clear gloss base, drip colors and stir until the shade matches the order, then shake in glitter and squeeze a yummy fruit flavor. Fill the tube to the line, click on a charm cap, add a sticker and test the gloss on big cute lips. Earn coins to unlock new tube shapes.

  • Real color mixing with a shade match meter
  • Glitter, fruit flavors, charm caps and stickers
  • Six cute tube shapes to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
