About the game

Visit a flower greenhouse and make perfume for your friends. Tap roses, lavender, lilies and fruits to pick them, grind the petals in a mortar and warm them into pure essence. Pour each scent to its line in a fancy bottle, tie a silk bow and add sparkling gems. Squeeze the atomizer and spray a cloud of petals and sparkles.

  • Pick flowers and fruits in a pretty garden
  • Mix up to three scents in the right parts
  • Six fancy bottles to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
