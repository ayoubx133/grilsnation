Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Crumble, roll, dip and decorate cute cake pops for animal party guests!

About the game

Rub the cake to crumble it, stir in frosting and roll soft little balls with your finger. Push in the sticks, then drag each pop into the glossy coating pot and watch it drip. Add bunny, bear, unicorn, kitty, panda or piggy faces and shake on sprinkles. Match each party order to earn stars and coins for new stick styles.

  • Crumble, mix, roll and dip with your finger
  • Six cute faces and four kinds of sprinkles
  • Party orders from adorable animal friends
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
