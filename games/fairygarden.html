Pixie Petal Garden

Build a tiny fairy garden and light it up at night with fireflies.

About the game

Pull the weeds, pick up twigs and sweep the dry leaves to clear the forest glade. Then place mushroom houses, flower patches, a pond, stepping stones, lanterns, a bridge, swings and fairy lights to match the theme card, and pick their colors. Tap the night button to see windows and fairy lights glow while fireflies dance. A little pixie friend flies along and you can unlock new pixie friends with coins.

  • Build cute fairy houses and gardens
  • Day and night with glowing lights and fireflies
  • Collect six pixie friends
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
