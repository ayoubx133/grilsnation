Party Costume Closet

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pick cute costumes for every party, then pose in the photo booth!

About the game

Every party has an invitation with wishes, like a spooky witch hat, bunny ears or a snowflake crown. Choose costumes such as a witch, a pumpkin, a snow queen, a bunny, a kitty or an angel, then add hats, crowns, props, shoes and hairstyles. Tap PARTY and tap the camera three times to snap three fun poses in the photo booth. The bunny, kitty and bear judges hold up their scores and you earn coins for new party decorations.

  • Six adorable party costumes with matching hats, ears and crowns
  • Seasonal party themes from a spooky party to a snow ball
  • Photo booth strip with three poses and friendly critter judges
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
