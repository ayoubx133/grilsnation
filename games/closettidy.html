Closet Tidy Up

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Fold, hang and pair cute clothes to turn a messy room into a dream closet.

About the game

Clothes are all over the floor and it is time to tidy up. Drag each dress or hoodie onto the hanger with the matching color bow, put crumpled tees on the folding mat and swipe to fold them, then slide them into their cubby. Drop a sneaker or sock onto its twin to make a pair and put everything in its place. Finish each closet to see a before and after photo of your work.

  • Relaxing folding, hanging and pairing in every closet
  • Before and after photo for each tidy closet
  • Six closet colors to unlock with your coins
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
