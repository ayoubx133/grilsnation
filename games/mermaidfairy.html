Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Switch between a cute anime mermaid and a flower fairy and dress her up!

About the game

Every level has a theme card that asks for a mermaid under the sea or a fairy in the flower forest, plus a few wishes like a gold tail or pink wings. Tap the big switch to turn her into a mermaid or a fairy with a magic swirl, then pick hairstyles, sparkly tails, shell tops, petal dresses, fluttering wings, crowns, pearls and wands. Tap DONE to take her photo and watch the cute bunny, kitty and bear judges hold up their scores. Earn coins to unlock magic hours like Sunset, Moonlight and Rainbow.

  • Two magical looks in one: mermaid tails and fairy wings
  • Shimmering tails, shell tops, petal dresses and fluttering wings
  • Painted sea palace and flower forest with bubbles and fireflies
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
