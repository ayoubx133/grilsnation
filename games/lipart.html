Paint cute lip art with your finger: colors, stencils, glitter and gems!

About the game

Every customer brings a look card with the lip art she wants. Pick a lipstick color and paint the lips with your finger, blend a dreamy ombre in the middle, rub color over a heart or star stencil and peel it off to reveal the pattern. Shake sparkly glitter, stick shiny lip gems and swipe on a glossy top coat, then see the before and after in the magic mirror.

  • Finger painting lip art with stencils, ombre and glitter
  • Dozens of look cards with stars to collect
  • Unlock six sparkly vanity mirrors
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
