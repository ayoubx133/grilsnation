Decorate a cute dollhouse room by room and meet the friends who move in.

About the game

Zoom into each room of a big cut-away dollhouse, swipe away the cobwebs and wipe off the dust. Drag sofas, beds, tables, lamps and plants into the room to match the wish card, and pick your favorite colors. When a room is finished a new chibi friend moves in with a short sweet story. Fill all six rooms, then start a brand new house in a new color.

  • Six rooms in every dollhouse
  • Cute chibi friends move in with little stories
  • Many furniture pieces and colors
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
