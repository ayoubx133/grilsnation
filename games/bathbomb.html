Mix, mold and pop out bath bombs, then watch them fizz in the tub!

About the game

Make colorful bath bombs step by step. Sift the powders, drip in colors and whisk until smooth, then sprinkle petals, glitter or stars. Scoop the mix into a heart, star or donut mold, press it tight and tap to pop it out. Drop it into the bathtub and swirl the water as it fizzes into dreamy colors with a cute bath toy.

  • Relaxing fizz and swirl bath time
  • Six molds and six fun add-ins
  • Unlock cute bath toys
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
