About the game

Pick a tutu, ribbon pointe shoes, a pretty hairstyle, a crown and a wand to match the show card. When the curtain opens, a gentle ballet waltz begins: tap the hearts on the ring, hold the ribbons and tap the stars to make her spin. Cute bunny, kitty and bear judges hold up their scores at the end. Collect coins to unlock new stage colors.

  • Six sparkly tutus with matching pointe shoes
  • Easy tap, hold and spin rhythm dancing
  • Original soft ballet music and cute critter judges
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
