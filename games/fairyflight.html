About the game

Tap to flutter your fairy through a magical night forest. Fly close to the paper lanterns to light them up, gather glowing fireflies that follow you like a sparkly trail and slip through the gaps in the thorny vines. Light every lantern on the way to the Moon Tree for three stars. Unlock new fairy wings and dresses with your coins.

  • Gentle tap to flutter flying
  • Lanterns that light up the dark forest
  • Six fairy wing and dress colors
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
