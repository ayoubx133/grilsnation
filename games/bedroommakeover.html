Cozy Bedroom Makeover

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Clean a messy bedroom, then decorate it with cute furniture and wallpaper.

About the game

Pick up the trash, rub away the dust and swipe off the cobwebs to make the room sparkle. Then drag beds, lamps, rugs, plants and posters into the room to match the theme card. Tap any piece to change its color, flip it or make it bigger, and choose a pretty wallpaper. Finish each room to see a before and after photo and earn stars and coins for new room colors.

  • Clean up trash, dust and cobwebs
  • 24 cute furniture pieces in many colors
  • Before and after photo for every room
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
