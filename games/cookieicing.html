Cookie Icing Art

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pipe, flood and decorate sugar cookies with hearts, pearls and sprinkles!

About the game

Pick a cookie shape and trace the outline with the piping bag. Fill it with smooth flood icing and watch it settle shiny and glossy. Drop dots of a second color on the wet icing and drag through them to make tiny hearts, then add pearls or sprinkles. Finish each bakery order and wrap your cookie in a pretty gift bag.

  • Real outline and flood icing with your finger
  • Wet on wet hearts that you pull yourself
  • Six cookie shapes and many icing colors
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
