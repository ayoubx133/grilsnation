Dress Designer Sketch

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Sketch, paint and sew your own dresses, then show them on the runway!

About the game

Trace the dress sketch with your pencil and watch a white dress appear. Paint every part with your brush, rub cute prints like dots, hearts and stars onto the fabric and sew the seams by following the stitch lines. Your model wears the new dress in the atelier show while the judges hold up their scores. Earn coins and unlock magic pencils.

  • Six dress shapes to sketch, paint and sew
  • Brush painting that keeps the fabric folds and shading
  • Atelier fashion show with photo and judges
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
