Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pour glossy nail polish colors until every bottle holds just one shade.

About the game

Tap a polish bottle to pop off its cap, then tap another bottle to pour the top color across. A color can only go onto the same color or into an empty bottle. Fill each bottle with one single shade and watch the cap twist back on with a sparkle. Use undo, hints or an extra bottle when you get stuck, and unlock cute bottle styles with your coins.

  • Over 100 calm color sorting levels with a gentle difficulty ramp
  • Mystery bottles with hidden colors to discover
  • Six cute bottle styles to unlock with coins
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
