Dress a cute paper doll with tab clothes and color your own paper dress!

About the game

Every level is an order card like Rainy Day, Tea Party or Snow Day. Drag paper clothes, hats, bags and shoes from the closet onto the paper doll and watch the little paper tabs fold over her shoulders. On art class days you pick crayons and color a blank paper dress any way you like, then it joins the closet as your very own design. Snap a photo on the craft desk and see the bunny, kitty and bear judges hold up their scores.

  • Paper clothes with real folding tabs
  • Color your own paper dress with crayons
  • Six patterned paper backdrops to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
