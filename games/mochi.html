Knead, fill and pinch squishy mochi with cute faces for your cafe friends!

About the game

Stretch and squish the soft rice dough, then knead in matcha, sakura or mango color. Roll it flat, add strawberry, cream, red bean or mango and pinch the edges closed. Dust it with powder and trace a cute face with the chocolate pen. Pack it into a bento box and make every animal customer smile.

  • Squishy dough that stretches and wobbles
  • Fillings, colors and faces you draw yourself
  • Cozy Japanese cafe with bento box orders
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
