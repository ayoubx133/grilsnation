Bloom Bouquet Shop

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Run a cute flower shop and arrange bouquets just the way customers ask!

About the game

Each customer brings an order card that shows how many roses, daisies, tulips or sunflowers they want, plus the wrap and ribbon colors. Pick a wrap paper, then drag flowers into the bouquet right where you like them, or tap a flower to add it. Tuck in soft greens, tie a pretty ribbon and add a sweet little card. Tap SERVE to see the order checked off and earn stars and coins for new wrap patterns.

  • Twelve hand drawn flowers and greens to arrange your own way
  • Customer orders with counting, colors and ribbons
  • Unlock polka dot, heart, star, stripe and gold lace wraps
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
