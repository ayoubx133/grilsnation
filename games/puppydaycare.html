About the game

Welcome to the sweetest puppy daycare in town! Each puppy shows a little bubble with what it wants right now: food, water, a walk in the garden, playtime with the toys or a nap in a comfy bed. Drag every puppy to the right spot before its bubble runs out and watch it munch, slurp, play and snooze. Happy puppies fill the happy meter and earn up to three stars. Days get busier as more puppies join, and nobody ever loses: a missed bubble only lowers the meter a little. Collect coins and dress your pups in collars, bows, bandanas, flowers, bow ties and crowns.

  • Drag and drop puppy care with five different needs
  • Up to four cute puppies at once in a cozy daycare
  • Collars, bows, bandanas and crowns to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
