Handbag Design Shop

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Dip, paint and decorate cute handbags and sell them in your own boutique!

About the game

Customers visit your boutique with an order card for their dream bag. Pick the bag shape, dip the white bag into colorful dye vats and dip again in another color for a pretty ombre. Paint the handles, snap on a golden clasp and hang a cute charm like a bunny, a pompom or a cherry. Sell the bag at the counter to earn coins and unlock new shop awnings.

  • Six bag shapes: tote, round, heart, backpack, clutch and bucket
  • Dip dye with real ombre effects plus handle painting
  • Golden clasps, cute charms and happy customers
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
