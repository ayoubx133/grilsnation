Sneaker Doodle Shop

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Spray paint, lace and decorate sneakers for cute customers in your shop!

About the game

Customers bring an order card for their dream sneakers. Spray paint the toe, side, heel, tongue and sole, lace the shoe up by tracing through the eyelets and flick colorful paint splashes. Drag cute stickers like hearts, stars, kitties and rainbows onto your design and show it on the pedestal. Happy customers give you coins to unlock new spray cans.

  • Spray paint every part of a high top sneaker
  • Lacing, paint splashes and twelve cute stickers
  • Fun orders from kid customers with star ratings
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
