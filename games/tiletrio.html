Tap stacked beauty tiles and match three of a kind in the tray.

About the game

Tap a tile that is not covered by another tile and it moves into the tray at the bottom. Collect three of the same beauty icon, like lipstick, perfume or a crown, and they pop away. The tray holds seven tiles, so plan your taps and clear the whole board. Use undo, hint or shuffle whenever you need a little help.

  • Layered boards full of cute beauty icons
  • Every board can be cleared, with undo, hint and shuffle helpers
  • Six tile styles to unlock with your coins
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
