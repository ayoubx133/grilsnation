Resin Charm Keychain

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pour, swirl and cure sparkly resin charms, then turn them into cute keychains.

About the game

Read the customer order and pick a heart, star, moon, kitty, flower or butterfly mold. Choose a resin color and hold to pour it up to the line, add a second layer and swirl the colors into marble with your finger. Shake in glitter and drop in tiny flowers, stars or hearts, hold the UV lamp until it turns glossy and pull the charm out of the mold. Clip on a keychain for a photo reveal and earn coins to unlock new keychain styles.

  • Hold to pour and swirl glossy resin
  • Glitter, flowers, stars and hearts inside
  • Six molds and six keychain styles
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
