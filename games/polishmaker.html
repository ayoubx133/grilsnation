Nail Polish Maker DIY

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Mix pigments, shake the bottle and paint a glossy nail wheel!

About the game

Make your own nail polish in a dreamy salon. Spoon pigment powders into the clear lacquer and watch the match dial as the colors swirl, then add shimmer, gold flakes or holo sparkles. Push the brush cap down, twist it shut and shake the bottle until the color is smooth. Paint the nails on the wheel with your finger and dry them under the lamp.

  • Pigment mixing with a match dial
  • Shimmer, flakes, glitter, stars and hearts
  • Six nail tip shapes to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
