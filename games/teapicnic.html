Teacup Picnic Party

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Brew tea, cut cute sandwiches and set a picnic for your animal friends!

About the game

Pick a tea, pour hot water into the glass teapot and stir until the color is just right. Stamp heart, star and bunny sandwiches with cookie cutters and spread jam on warm scones. Drag the tea set onto the picnic blanket and pour a cup for every guest. Then watch your animal friends hop in for the party!

  • Brew, cut, spread and pour with your finger
  • Set a pretty picnic blanket your way
  • Cute animal friends arrive for tea time
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
