Curly Cuts Salon

Wash, cut, color and curl cute anime hair to match every client order!

About the game

Every client brings an order card, like a short cut, pink color, curly style and a bow clip. Rub in shampoo foam and spray it away, comb out the tangles, then swipe the scissors across the hair to snip it to the right length. Paint on bright hair dye, use the curling iron for springy curls or the dryer for a sleek shine, and drag sparkly clips anywhere you like. Snap a photo in the salon, earn stars and coins, and unlock new capes for your clients.

  • Cut, wash, comb, dye and curl with your finger
  • Six dye colors, curly or sleek styles and cute hair clips
  • Fun client orders with stars and six capes to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
