About the game

A muddy puppy or kitten comes to your pet salon with an order from its owner. Rub in the bubble foam, spray it all away, rub the fur dry with a fluffy towel, brush out the knots and blow dry until the fur is extra fluffy. Then drag on the bows, crowns, collars, raincoats and tutus from the order and watch your happy pet wiggle and throw hearts. Earn coins to unlock new bathtubs and cushions.

  • Real finger grooming: foam, rinse, towel, brush and blow dry
  • Adorable anime puppy and kitten that react to every step
  • Bows, crowns, collars, a raincoat and a tutu to match each order
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
