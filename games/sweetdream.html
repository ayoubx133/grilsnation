About the game

Every customer brings an order photo of the dessert they dream of. Pick the cake, cupcake, donut, macaron, pudding or ice cream, then swirl on frosting with your finger, shake sprinkles where you like and place yummy toppings and candles. Serve it and earn up to three stars for matching the order photo. Collect coins to unlock pretty cake stands.

  • Frost with your finger and shake sprinkles anywhere
  • Six desserts, six frosting colors and lots of cute toppings
  • Cheerful music box tunes in a cozy pink bakery
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
