Swim with a cute mermaid, collect pearls and dodge jellyfish and nets!

About the game

Drag up and down to guide the mermaid through the sparkling sea. Follow the pearl trails, grab golden rings for a speedy boost and swim around jellyfish, fishing nets and grumpy pufferfish. Reach the giant clam at the end of each swim. Pearls turn into coins that unlock new rainbow, coral, violet and golden tails.

  • Smooth one finger swimming
  • Pearls, boost rings and heart bubbles
  • Six colorful mermaid tails to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
