Cozy Candle Craft

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Melt, color and pour cozy candles in cute jars and teacups, then light them up.

About the game

Read the customer order and pick a jar, teacup, pot, shell, star tin or tall glass. Drop wax flakes into the pot and hold to melt them, add a color chip and stir in circles, then add a sweet scent. Place the wick in the middle and hold to pour the wax up to the line, with two colored layers on later orders. Sprinkle dried flowers on top, strike a match and watch the room glow, then earn coins to unlock new flame colors.

  • Melt, stir and pour real looking wax
  • Six cute containers and layered candles
  • Light it up for a cozy glowing photo
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
