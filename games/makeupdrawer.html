About the game

Drag a lipstick, blush, perfume or brush into another drawer. Put three of the same item together in one drawer and they pop away with a sparkle. More makeup waits at the back of each drawer and slides forward when the front is empty. Clear every drawer as fast as you can to earn three stars, and use undo or hints whenever you need a little help.

  • Hundreds of cute cosmetics to sort in a pink vanity organizer
  • Every level is always solvable with a gentle difficulty ramp
  • Six drawer colors to unlock with your coins
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
