Bridal Gown Atelier

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Design dreamy fairy tale wedding gowns and dress the bride for her garden day!

About the game

Each bride brings a dream card for her fairy tale gown. Pick the gown shape, brush a soft pastel tint over the white dress, stitch lace along the hem by following the dots and tap pearls onto the bodice. Choose the veil, a pearl tiara or flower crown, a bouquet and heels. Then the bride walks down the garden aisle under falling petals for a sweet photo at the flower arch.

  • Five fairy tale gowns with pastel tints, lace and pearls
  • Veils, tiaras, flower crowns, bouquets and heels
  • Garden aisle walk with petals and a photo
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
