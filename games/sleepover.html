About the game

Every party night is a row of fun mini games. Stack pillows into a cozy fort, catch popping popcorn in a bowl, smooth and peel a face mask, paint pretty nails, have a soft pillow fight and connect the stars to draw shapes in the sky. Each night ends with stargazing and lights out. Earn coins to unlock new pillow colors.

  • Six different sleepover mini games
  • A new mix of games every night
  • Cozy pajama party music and cute friends
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
