Make cute bubble tea drinks for happy customers in a cozy anime cafe.

About the game

Read the customer order card, then pick the cup and hold to pour tea and milk right up to the line. Tap to drop in pearls, jelly, berries and ice, swirl whipped cream with your finger, press the lid until it clicks and poke in the straw. Serve the drink and watch your customer smile, then earn up to three stars for matching the order. Collect coins to unlock cute cup stickers.

  • Hold to pour, swirl cream and shake drinks with your finger
  • Six cute cups, six tea flavors and four yummy toppings
  • Relaxing cafe music and satisfying sounds
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
