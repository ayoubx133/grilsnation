About the game

Drag the everyday things from the tray into the bag organizer and fit them all in with no gaps. Tap a piece to turn it so it fits the space you need. Every bag can be packed perfectly, from a mini purse to a big suitcase. When everything is inside, drag the zipper pull across to zip the bag shut.

  • Cute bags to pack: handbag, backpack, suitcase, tote and more
  • Relaxing block fitting puzzles that grow slowly in size
  • Six lining patterns and zipper charms to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
