About the game

Your client brings a photo of the rainbow hair look she dreams of. Comb her long hair into strands, pick a chalk color and rub it along each strand, from full color to tips only and ombre looks. Seal the colors with hair spray and sprinkle glitter when she asks for it. The closer you match the photo, the more stars you get!

  • Rub real chalk color onto each strand
  • Full, tips, ombre and rainbow looks
  • Six chalk palettes from pastel to neon
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
