Pull, snip, fold and pinch ribbon into cute bows for every hairstyle.

About the game

Open your own bow boutique and make hair bows by hand. Pick the ribbon your customer wants, pull it from the roll and snip it, drag both ends to the middle to fold the loops, then press and hold to pinch the knot. Decorate with pearls, hearts, stars and diamonds and place the bow on the model's hairstyle for a photo. Satin, polka dot, glitter, lace and velvet ribbons are waiting!

  • Make bows step by step with your finger
  • Five ribbon styles and sparkly gems
  • Six hairstyles to dress up
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
