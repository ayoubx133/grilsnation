About the game

Every level is a celebration card with a wish list. Roll a fabric bolt across the white kaftan to give it a beautiful color, then follow the dotted lines with the golden thread and watch the shiny embroidery appear. Drag a belt onto the waist, pick pretty jewels and pose for a photo in a glowing hall full of lanterns and roses. Earn coins to unlock new golden needles.

  • Six elegant kaftan styles in eight fabric colors
  • Golden thread tracing with a shimmering embroidery reveal
  • Belts, jewels and a celebration photo
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
